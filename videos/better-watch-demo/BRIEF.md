## Intent

Create a production-ready, sub-one-minute demo of Better Watch / Ultra Touch Library. The video
should feel calm, precise, and useful: start with the problem of a scattered external-drive
library, then show the product turning it into an organized workspace without hype.

## Assets

- Source UI: ../../src/main.jsx and ../../public/styles.css — product behavior and visual language.
- assets/better-watch-mark.svg — neutral wordmark mark for the opening and closing lockup.

## Customizations

- Feature the real product flow: collection navigation, search and sort, hover preview, details,
  Favorites / Watch Later / Watched marks, and the Artists view.
- Use sanitized neutral preview artwork and fictional, non-sensitive titles in the demo frames.
- Keep the demo in 16:9, under one minute, English, and silent by default.

## Notes

- Do not show real library thumbnails, private filenames, explicit content, or invented metrics.
- The product UI is reconstructed in the composition so the visual proof stays deterministic and
  safe to share while preserving the actual interaction model.
