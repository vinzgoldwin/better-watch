## Video direction

- Palette: warm cream and paper surfaces from `frame.md`, ink for the product UI, and one red
  accent for active states and the play action. Keep the product surface recognizable while the
  surrounding title cards use the same warm editorial system.
- Motion grammar: smooth long-tail arrivals, crisp UI state changes, and sequential reveals paced
  to each title cue. Use one camera move per section at most; let the UI carry the proof. Holds are
  deliberate on the overview and closing plates.
- Rhythm: the opening and close breathe; the middle is a measured feature relay. Frames 02 and 08
  are held reads. Frames 03–07 reveal one UI capability at a time instead of dumping the whole
  surface on the first beat.
- Negative list: no explicit or private media, no browser chrome, no invented metrics, no fake
  customer logos, no purple/blue gradients, no bouncing UI, no screensaver drift, and no
  front-loaded slideshow motion.
- Safe area: keep essential text and UI proof above the bottom caption band even though this cut is
  silent and ships without captions.
